The Buying Process

Your Home Buying Journey

Buying a home involves many decisions along the way. Mike can help you understand each stage and move through the process with greater clarity and confidence.

01

Define Your Goals

Start by identifying your priorities, preferred locations, budget, timeline, and the type of home that best fits your needs.

02

Before You Buy

What to Consider Before Buying a Home

A successful home purchase involves more than finding a property you like. Understanding your budget, ongoing ownership costs, location priorities and the condition of the home can help you make a more informed decision.

Mike can help you evaluate properties in context, understand important considerations, and identify questions worth asking before you move forward.

Discuss Your Home Search
01

Budget Beyond the Purchase Price

Consider closing costs, property taxes, utilities, insurance, maintenance and other ongoing expenses.

02

Mortgage & Financing

Understand your financing options and how your approved budget fits with your overall financial plans.

03

Location & Lifestyle

Think about commute, schools, amenities, neighbourhood characteristics and how the location fits your day-to-day needs.

04

Property Condition

Look beyond appearance and consider the age, condition and maintenance needs of the home and its major systems.

05

Condo Fees & Ownership Costs

For condominium properties, review monthly fees and understand what services and expenses are included.

06

Long-Term Fit

Consider whether the property and location are likely to continue meeting your needs as your circumstances change.
