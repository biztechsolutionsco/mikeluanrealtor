Preparing to Sell

A Thoughtful Approach From the Start

Selling a home involves more than placing a property on the market. Preparation, pricing, presentation and timing can all influence how buyers respond to your listing.

Mike works with sellers to understand their priorities, evaluate the property and develop a strategy suited to the home and current market conditions.
